# about

I mostly work in the gap between writing something and actually shooting it. A script tells you what a scene is supposed to mean. The camera and the edit decide whether anyone feels it.

So far that has been: a 23-page non-linear short I wrote and directed in Chennai, a year in Goa doing world-building and structure work on a narrative universe called MAYA, and now a video essay channel I'm making on my own. Before any of that I did competitive debate and Model UN for years, which taught me roughly the same thing from a different direction - how you stage an argument matters about as much as the argument.

  • 32 MUN conferences
  • 13x best delegate
  • 2021 national debate champion
  • 23 pages, shot and cut
